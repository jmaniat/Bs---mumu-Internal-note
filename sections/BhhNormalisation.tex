\subsection{\Bhh Normalisation}
\label{subsec:BhhNormalisation}

To accurately measure the \Bd signal yield, which could otherwise be unrealistically inflated (or deflated) by the \Bhh and \Bdmumu invariant mass overlap when fitted to data, the \Bhh normalisation is constrained in the signal channel fit.
Hence, a realistic estimate of this background yield is required.
The yield is estimated using a data-driven method. 
First, the equivalent of equation~\ref{eq:BRFormula} for \Bhh in place of \Bmumu is used together with \Bhh MC simulation to compute an MC-based prediction of the \Bhh yield. 
The normalisation of this MC-based prediction is then adjusted by comparing it to a fit to data in an unblinded control regions which features the same selections as in the signal channel but requires the muons to fail the tight-ID WP, thus being orthogonal to the main SR. 
Such regions/muons are referred to as passing Loose-Not-Tight (LNT) requirements since in all cases the muons are required to still pass the Loose ID WP. 
The procedure is validated using a second control region in which only one of the muons is required to be LNT. 
Details of each of these aspects is covered in the following subsections.

\subsubsection{Fit to LNT-LNT Control Region}
\label{subsubsec:BhhDataFits}

The LNT-LNT data region is fit with an adjusted version of the baseline model described in section~\ref{subsec:FitConfig}. 
The adjustments to the fit model include:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Fixing the \Bs (\Bd) model parameters to the results of (simultaneous extended unbinned maximum likelihood) \DJcomment{fits to MC in the LNT-LNT region}{Good to put in an appendix}. 
  \item Fixing the \Bhh shape parameters to the those detailed in section~\ref{subsubsec:BhhFunctionalForm}
  \item Fixing the non-resonant background model parameters to the result of the data sidebands fit in the T-T region in section~\ref{subsubsec:SimultaneousSSSVplusCOMBcombinedFitMCandDataSB}
  \item Leaving the \Bhh normalisation free
  \item Constraining the signal yields to the simulation + SM based estimates of Table~\ref{tab:MasterEqEstMuonWPs}
\end{itemize}

Figure~\ref{fig:LNT-LNTdatafit} shows the result of the LNT-LNT data fit with the adjusted model described earlier in this section. Visually, the fitted shape reproduces the data well and with a reasonable fit quality in each BDT bin. The goodness of fit was evaluated using the Baker-Cousins (BC) $\chi^{2}$,  $\chi^{2}_{BC}$ due to the low statistics. \footnote{The fit quality for low statistic cases, like the LNT-LNT data fit, is evaluated using a different statistic than the Pearson $\chi^2$, which breaks down at low event counts where the $\chi^{2}$ distribution approximation no longer holds. Instead, the Baker-Cousins $\chi^{2}$ ($\chi^{2}_{BC}$) statistic is used since it incorporates the exact Poisson likelihood and hence can accommodate the low statistics Poisson regime. However, the $\chi^{2}_{BC}$ does not follow standard $\chi^{2}$ distribution with a well defined number of degrees of freedom so analytical methods of approximating the p-value with this test statistic are unsuitable. Instead the frequentist toy MC based approach is used in calculation of the p-values. The p-value for the goodness of fit is calculated as the fraction of toy MC datasets generated according to the fitted model that provide a $\chi^{2}_{BC}$ that is higher than or equal to the one observed in the fit to the original dataset. This method will hence forth be used in the goodness of fit p-value evaluations of low statistic sample fits due to its reliability in such regimes.}, and
The resulting p-values are satisfactory in each of the BDT bins. 
The \Bhh yield was found to be $N^{LNT-LNT}_{hh'} = 13.8 \pm 5.5$, consistent with the $21.0 \pm 5.5$ expected events from the MC-based prediction.

\subsubsection{Extrapolation to Signal Region}
\label{subsubsec:BhhExtrapolation}

The \Bhh yield from the data fit in the LNT-LNT region is used to adjust the MC-based prediction for the T-T SR using the following equation:
\begin{eqnarray}
  N_{hh'}^{T-T} = \frac{MC_{hh'}^{T-T}}{MC_{hh'}^{LNT-LNT}} \times N_{hh'}^{LNT-LNT}
  %= (0.64 \pm 0.17) \times (13.4 \pm 5.5) = (8.6 \pm 4.1) \text{ events} % before updated signal estimates constraints
  = (0.64 \pm 0.17) \times (13.8 \pm 5.5) = (8.8 \pm 4.2) \text{ events}.
   % after updated signal estimates constraints (MC ratio instead of muon efficiencies from TT to WP)
  \label{eq:T-T_LNT-LNT_dataBhhYield} 
\end{eqnarray}
Here, the MC values use all available events \DJcomment{including those outside the mass fit range.}{Does this mean the full or wide mass range? I think we want to use the wide mass range for this.}
This data normalised prediction is used for the signal channel fits, though it should be notes that it is consistent with the MC-based expectation of $13.39 \pm 0.56$ events. 


\subsubsection{Validation in LNT-T + T-LNT Region}
\label{subsubsec:BhhValidationFits}

As a loose cross-check of the procedure used to translate the LNT-LNT data \Bhh yield into the T-T region, the LNT-LNT data \Bhh yield is translated into the T-LNT + LNT-T region, which is then compared to the result of a direct fit to the T-LNT + LNT-T data. 
For the T-LNT + LNT-T data fit, the \Bhh yield is constrained to the result of the LNT-LNT data \Bhh yield translated into the T-LNT + LNT-T region as a means of assessing the compatibility of the scaled result in data. 
The constraint applied is
\begin{eqnarray}
  N_{hh'}^{T-LNT+LNT-T} = \frac{MC_{hh'}^{T-LNT+LNT-T}}{MC_{hh'}^{LNT-LNT}} \times N_{hh'}^{LNT-LNT}
  %= (1.46 \pm 0.31) \times (13.4 \pm 5.5) = (19.6 \pm 9.0) \text{ events} % before updated signal estimates constraints
  = (1.46 \pm 0.31) \times (13.8 \pm 5.5) = (20.2 \pm 9.0) \text{ events} % after updated signal estimates constraints (MC ratio instead of muon efficiencies from TT to WP)
  \label{eq:T-LNT-LNT-T_LNT-LNT_dataBhhYield} 
\end{eqnarray}

Figure~\ref{fig:T-LNT-LNT-Tdatafit} shows the result of the T-LNT + LNT-T data fit with the constraint on the \Bhh yield from equation~\ref{eq:T-LNT-LNT-T_LNT-LNT_dataBhhYield}. 
The fit reproduces the data reasonably well with a satisfactory quality of the fit in each BDT bin, serving as a validation of the predicted \Bhh yield.  
The fitted \Bhh yield, with the above constraint applied, was found to be $N^{T-LNT+LNT-T}_{hh'} = 9.8 \pm 8.0$.  \footnote{The ratio $\frac{N_{hh'}^{LNT-LNT}}{N_{hh'}^{T-LNT+LNT-T}} = 1.4 \pm 1.3$ ($0.69 \pm 0.25$ expected) \textcolor{red}{(PR2: $1.17 ± 1.6$)} measures directly the single-leg hadron species-averaged fake rate for the \Bhh daughters. 
From the data measurements, the estimation of the species-averaged per-leg T vs L relative fake rate $\frac{N_{hh'}^{T-LNT+LNT-T}}{N_{hh'}^{T-LNT+LNT-T} + 2 \times N_{hh'}^{LNT-LNT}} = 0.26 \pm 0.18$ ($0.421 \pm 0.087$ expected) \textcolor{red}{(PR2: $0.299 \pm 0.287$)} was measured. \textcolor{red}{All this needs to be checked, since for T-LNT+LNT-T data fit used in these equations we used a constraint on the Bhh yield as mentioned in text, expectations are from the table~\ref{tab:MasterEqEstMuonWPs}.}}.
