\subsection{Triggers}
\label{subsec:Triggers}

The triggers used for the signal and reference channels are shown in Table~\ref{tab:triggers_signal} with their recorded integrated luminosities. 
For 2015 and 2016, the triggers used are the same ones as in the Partial Run 2 analysis, while for 2017 and 2018 new triggers were developed which required topological cuts at Level 1, in order to maintain manageable rates with the increased instantaneous luminosity.
For 2017 and 2018, the triggers requiring muons with \pT above 6~\GeV are active for most of the data taking, while the triggers with lower \pT thresholds are prescaled in the sense that they were only activated (without a prescale) towards the end of fills.
