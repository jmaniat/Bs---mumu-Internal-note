\section{Data and Monte Carlo Samples}

\subsection{Data Samples}
\label{sec:DataSamples}

The analysis uses the data collected by the ATLAS experiment between 2015-2018. 
For un-prescaled triggers, this corresponds to an integrated luminosity of $140 \pm 1.2\,\ifb$~\cite{ATLAS:2022hro}.  
\textred{Check data quality requirements (Event Cleaning).}

As the analysis makes use of combined muons, the data events used are required to come from runs in which the Inner Detector and Muon Spectrometer were fully operational. 
The recommended Good Run Lists (GRLs) are used and are listed for each data period are listed in Table~\ref{tab:GRLs}.
