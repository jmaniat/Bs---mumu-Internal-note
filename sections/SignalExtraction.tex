\section{\Bs and \Bd Signal Extraction}
\label{sec:SignalExtraction}

In order to extract the \Bds signal yields, an extended simultaneous unbinned maximum-likelihood fit is perfomed on the invariant mass distribution of \Bds events that are classified according to differet intervals in the BDT output. This strategy is similar to the one followed by \lhcb \textcolor{red}{(add citation)} and \cms \textcolor{red}{(add citation)}, along with the \atlas Partial Run 2 version of the analysis \textcolor{red}{(add citation)}. Unless otherwise stated, throughout this chapter we assume the latest SM branching fraction predictions \BRBdmumu = $3.66 \times 10^{-9}$ and \BRBsmumu = $1.03 \times 10^{-10}$ as mentioned in Section~\ref{sec::intro}. To prevent introduction of biases or double counting effects during the development of the fitting procedure, MC samples have been used for preparatory studies and where possible verified on data sidebands.

Two classifications of \Bds events in BDT output are being investigated: the default and alternative BDT output binnings.
